Yuchen Jiao

 

I am a Research Associate in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Professor Gen Li.

Previously, I received my Ph.D. in the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University, advised by Professor Yuantao Gu. Before my Ph.D., I received my bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2018.

I am interested in diffusion-based generative models, reinforcement learning, transformers, and signal processing.
